<core-menu> composable event

Dropdown menu with a trigger label and live <core-menu-item>, <core-menu-separator>, and optional <core-menu-submenu> children. The root panel opens below the trigger and flips above at the viewport edge. A submenu opens to the right of its item and flips left when needed. Opening another core-menu in the same document closes the previous root and its whole submenu chain. A submenu stays closed while any ancestor is closed or disabled.

Tag / attributeValuesDescription
core-menu · labelstringTrigger text (required).
core-menu · alignstart · endHorizontal panel alignment relative to the trigger (default: end). Vertical placement flips automatically at viewport edges.
core-menu · openbooleanShow / hide panel.
core-menu · disabledbooleanDisable trigger.
core-menu-item · valuestringItem key (required).
core-menu-item · labelstringStatic fallback when light-DOM body is empty.
core-menu-item · disabledbooleanSkip item in keyboard nav and block selection.
core-menu-item · data-core-langJSONOn the host; child defaults to the inner label hook.
core-menu-separator—Horizontal rule between items (role="separator").
core-menu-submenu · labelstringMenuitem label. Updating it does not rebuild the panel.
core-menu-submenu · disabledbooleanSkips the entry and keeps its chain closed. The trigger button stays in sync without a rebuild.
core-menu-submenu · openbooleanReflects visibility. Ignored while an ancestor is closed or disabled.

Events: core-menu-select — detail.value. Also core-menu-open / core-menu-close.

Methods: openMenu(), closeMenu(), toggleMenu(), setLabel(label), refresh() (after dynamic children). On core-menu-submenu: openSubmenu(), closeSubmenu().

Behavior: one open core-menu root per document. Opening another root closes the previous root and every submenu under it, without returning focus to the previous trigger. Submenus of the same panel are exclusive with each other; opening one does not close its ancestors. A leaf selection emits one core-menu-select on the root. Distinct documents stay independent. There is no hover-to-open.

Keyboard: root trigger — ↓ / Enter / Space opens; ↑ opens and focuses the last item. Inside a panel — ↑↓ move, Home / End. → / Enter / Space on a submenu opens it and focuses its first item. ← or Escape closes one level and restores that level’s trigger. Tab, an outside click, or a leaf selection closes the whole chain and restores the root trigger.

User menu (align end)

Selected action will appear here.
<core-menu label="Alex" align="end">
  <core-menu-item value="profile" label="Profile"></core-menu-item>
  <core-menu-separator></core-menu-separator>
  <core-menu-item value="logout" label="Sign out"></core-menu-item>
</core-menu>

Single-open coordination

Open Menu A, then Menu B — A closes so only one menu stays open in this document.

Open a menu — the other closes.
<core-menu label="Menu A">…</core-menu>
<core-menu label="Menu B">…</core-menu>

Submenus

Open File, then Share, then More. Choosing SMS closes the chain and reports one value. A submenu does not open while File is closed.

Open Share, then More.
<core-menu label="File" align="start">
  <core-menu-item value="new" label="New"></core-menu-item>
  <core-menu-submenu label="Share">
    <core-menu-item value="mail" label="Email"></core-menu-item>
    <core-menu-submenu label="More">
      <core-menu-item value="sms" label="SMS"></core-menu-item>
    </core-menu-submenu>
  </core-menu-submenu>
</core-menu>
